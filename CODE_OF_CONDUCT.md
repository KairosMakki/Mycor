# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment and abuse are not tolerated.

## Our standards

- Be welcoming and inclusive.
- Assume good faith; ask for clarification.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
